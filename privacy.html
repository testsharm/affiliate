Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short: there are no accounts, forms or sign-ups on this site, and we do not ask for your personal details. A few small things are stored in your own browser, our hosting provider keeps normal technical logs, and optional analytics may count visits. When you click through to Whop, their rules apply.

1. Who we are

this site is a small independent website. Contact: [add your contact email in Admin, Site settings]

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: none through the site. We do not have sign-up, comment or contact forms. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.
  • Stored in your browser: a short list of prompts you have opened (so we can show "Viewed" and "Pick up where you left off") and the numbers you type into the cost check while the page is open. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Technical data from hosting: our hosting provider, Vercel, processes standard request data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested to deliver and secure the site. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Analytics (only if switched on): we may use a privacy-focused analytics tool (Umami) to count page views, which prompts are opened or copied, and clicks on promotional links. It is designed not to use cookies or track you across other sites. See that provider's documentation. If analytics is not switched on, none of this happens.

3. Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. The admin area, which only the site owner uses, sets a login cookie for the owner. When you click an affiliate link, the destination (Whop and the seller) may set cookies or similar tools so that a referral can be credited to us. That happens on their site and is covered by their policies.

4. Links to other sites

The site links to ToolSuite on Whop and may link to other sites. We do not control them. Please read their terms and privacy policies before you buy or share information.

5. How we use data

Only to run and secure the site, understand which pages are useful, and measure how many referral clicks we send. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we collect almost no personal data, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you can contact us at any time. You can also block cookies, clear stored data, or use browser privacy tools.

7. Children

The site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Security and retention

We use HTTPS and limit access to the admin area with a password. Technical logs and analytics are kept only as long as our providers keep them under their settings. No system is perfectly secure.
